    Big picture

    The Declaration was both an ending and a beginning

    Break It publicly severed the colonies from British rule.

    The Declaration turned rebellion into a formal argument for independence.

    War aim It gave the Revolution a clear purpose people could defend.

    Colonists were no longer just protesting policy. They were building a new nation.

    Legacy Its principles continued into the Constitution and Bill of Rights.

    The later framework of government was built to protect the rights and consent the Declaration announced.

    Consent of the governed as a feedback loop

    1

    People hold rights

    Rights are not granted by officials; government is built to secure them.

    2

    People give consent

    Voting, speech, juries, town halls, and petitions send public input into the system.

    3

    Power is checked

    When leaders drift from consent, elections and constitutional limits correct course.

    "Governments are instituted among Men, deriving their just powers from the consent of the governed."
